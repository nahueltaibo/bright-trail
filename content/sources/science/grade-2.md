## Strand A: STEM Skills and Connections

### Why is this learning important?

In all grades of the science and technology curriculum, the learning related to the STEM Skills and Connections area is applied to learning related to the other four areas of the curriculum.

### A1. STEM Investigation and Communication Skills

**A1.** use a scientific research process, a scientific experimentation process, and an engineering design process to conduct investigations, following appropriate health and safety procedures

#### A1.1

**A1.1** use a scientific research process and associated skills to conduct investigations

#### A1.2

**A1.2** use a scientific experimentation process and associated skills to conduct investigations

#### A1.3

**A1.3** use an engineering design process and associated skills to design, build, and test devices, models, structures, and/or systems

#### A1.4

**A1.4** follow established health and safety procedures during science and technology investigations, including wearing appropriate protective equipment and clothing and safely using tools, instruments, and materials

#### A1.5

**A1.5** communicate their findings, using science and technology vocabulary and formats that are appropriate for specific audiences and purposes

### A2. Coding and Emerging Technologies

**A2.** use coding in investigations and to model concepts, and assess the impact of coding and of emerging technologies on everyday life

#### A2.1

**A2.1** write and execute code in investigations and when modelling concepts, with a focus on decomposing problems into smaller steps

#### A2.2

**A2.2** identify and describe impacts of coding and of emerging technologies on everyday life

#### A3.1

#### A3.2

**A3.2** investigate how science and technology can be used with other subject areas to address real-world problems

#### A3.3

**A3.3** analyse contributions to science and technology from various communities

## Strand B: Life Systems

### Why is this learning important?

Students learn that animals grow and change throughout their lifecycles and that animals have distinct characteristics such as scales on fish, fur on cats, feathers on birds and shells on turtles. As students research animals in their local environment, they begin to acknowledge the interconnectedness of animals and the natural environment in which they live. They build their understanding of the effects humans have on animals and learn how they can exercise care and respect for animals and their natural environment or habitats.

### B1. Relating Science and Technology to Our Changing World

**B1.** assess ways in which animals have an impact on society and the environment, and ways in which human activities have an impact on animals and the places where they live

#### B1.1 Growth and Changes in Animals

**B1.1** examine impacts that animals can have on society and the environment, and describe some ways in which any negative impacts can be minimized

#### B1.2 Growth and Changes in Animals

**B1.2** assess impacts of various human activities on animals and the places where they live, and describe practices that can minimize negative impacts

### B2. Exploring and Understanding Concepts

**B2.** demonstrate an understanding that animals grow and change and have distinct characteristics

#### B2.1 Growth and Changes in Animals

**B2.1** compare physical characteristics of various animals, including characteristics that are constant and those that change

#### B2.2 Growth and Changes in Animals

**B2.2** describe the locomotion of various animals

#### B2.3 Growth and Changes in Animals

**B2.3** describe the life cycle of a variety of animals, including insects, amphibians, birds, and mammals

#### B2.4 Growth and Changes in Animals

**B2.4** compare changes in the appearance and behaviour of various animals as they go through a complete life cycle

#### B2.5 Growth and Changes in Animals

**B2.5** describe adaptations, including physical and/or behavioural characteristics, that allow various animals to survive in their natural environment

## Strand C: Matter and Energy

### Why is this learning important?

Students learn about the properties and physical changes of liquids and solids. They deepen their learning by testing various structures and the interaction or changes between solids and liquids. They also assess ways in which liquids and solids and their uses can have an impact on society and the environment.

### C1. Relating Science and Technology to Our Changing World

**C1.** assess ways in which liquids and solids and their uses can have an impact on society and the environment

#### C1.1 Properties of Liquids and Solids

**C1.1** assess practices related to the use, storage, and disposal of liquids and solids in the home in terms of the effects on personal health and safety and on the environment, and suggest ways to improve these practices

#### C1.2 Properties of Liquids and Solids

**C1.2** assess the impacts of changes of state of liquids and solids on humans and on environments

### C2. Exploring and Understanding Concepts

**C2.** demonstrate an understanding of the properties and physical changes of liquids and solids

#### C2.1 Properties of Liquids and Solids

**C2.1** identify various types of matter in natural and built environments as liquids or solids

#### C2.2 Properties of Liquids and Solids

**C2.2** describe the properties of liquids and solids

#### C2.3 Properties of Liquids and Solids

**C2.3** describe properties of liquid water and solid water, and identify the conditions that cause changes from one state to the other

#### C2.4 Properties of Liquids and Solids

**C2.4** identify conditions in which the states of liquids and solids remain constant and conditions that can cause their states to change

#### C2.5 Properties of Liquids and Solids

**C2.5** describe some ways in which liquids and solids can be combined to make useful mixtures

#### C2.6 Properties of Liquids and Solids

**C2.6** classify solid objects and materials in terms of their buoyancy and in terms of their ability to absorb or repel water

#### C2.7 Properties of Liquids and Solids

**C2.7** explain the meaning of international symbols that give us information on the safety of substances

## Strand D: Structures and Mechanisms

### Why is this learning important?

Students explore the uses of simple machines by designing and testing simple machines, such as a moving toy with a wheel and axle. They also begin to understand the importance of simple machines that can be found in their homes and communities, such as a pencil sharpener, a pair of scissors, a wheelbarrow, or a ramp.

### D1. Relating Science and Technology to Our Changing World

**D1.** assess the impact of simple machines on society and on the environment

#### D1.1 Simple Machines and Movement

**D1.1** assess the impact of simple machines on the daily lives of people in various communities

#### D1.2 Simple Machines and Movement

**D1.2** assess the impact on the environment of technologies that use simple machines to facilitate movement

### D2. Exploring and Understanding Concepts

**D2.** demonstrate an understanding of movement and ways in which simple machines help to move objects

#### D2.1 Simple Machines and Movement

**D2.1** describe different ways an object can move

#### D2.2 Simple Machines and Movement

**D2.2** identify ways in which the position of an object can be changed

#### D2.3 Simple Machines and Movement

**D2.3** identify the six basic types of simple machines: lever, inclined plane, wedge, pulley, wheel and axle, and screw

#### D2.4 Simple Machines and Movement

**D2.4** describe ways in which each type of simple machine is used in daily life to make tasks easier

#### D2.5 Simple Machines and Movement

**D2.5** compare, qualitatively or quantitatively, the force required to move an object using various simple machines to the force required to move the object without using a simple machine

## Strand E: Earth and Space Systems

### Why is this learning important?

Students begin to explore the properties of air and water, including water in its solid, liquid, and gaseous states in the environment. Students pursue their understanding of the importance of air and water by examining ways in which living things depend on air and water for their survival, such as that humans and other living things need air to survive, and that fish need clean water to thrive. Students develop an awareness that some communities do not have safe drinking water in their taps and learn that water is a resource that humans should use responsibly.

### E1. Relating Science and Technology to Our Changing World

**E1.** assess ways in which the actions of humans have an impact on the quality of air and water, and create plans to protect these resources

#### E1.1 Air and Water in the Environment

**E1.1** assess the impact of human activities on air and water, taking various perspectives into consideration, including those of First Nations, Métis, and Inuit, and plan a course of action to protect the quality of the air and/or water in the local community

#### E1.2 Air and Water in the Environment

**E1.2** assess their personal and household uses of water, and create a plan to use water responsibly

#### E1.3 Air and Water in the Environment

**E1.3** examine the availability of fresh water and drinking water around the world, and describe the impact on communities

### E2. Exploring and Understanding Concepts

**E2.** demonstrate an understanding of the properties of air and water, including water in various states, and of ways in which living things depend on air and water for their survival

#### E2.1 Air and Water in the Environment

**E2.1** demonstrate an understanding of the key properties of air and water

#### E2.2 Air and Water in the Environment

**E2.2** identify sources of water in the natural and built environments

#### E2.3 Air and Water in the Environment

**E2.3** describe the stages of the water cycle, including evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and collection

#### E2.4 Air and Water in the Environment

**E2.4** identify the three states of water in the environment, and describe how temperature changes affect the state of water within the water cycle

#### E2.5 Air and Water in the Environment

**E2.5** describe ways in which living things, including humans, depend on air and water
