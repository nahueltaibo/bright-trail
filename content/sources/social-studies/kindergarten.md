## Strand D: Belonging and Contributing

### Why is this learning important?

Your child will develop a sense of self. Educators will encourage and support them to reach their full potential. They will develop social skills, self-confidence and a love for learning that they will take with them through school and life.

Your child will develop:

- a positive understanding of themselves as a person and their unique strengths
- a sense of connectedness to others, to their community and to the natural world
- ways to consider different perspectives and life experiences as well as areas of connection
- understanding of relationships and community and how people contribute to the world around them
- problem-solving skills in social situations

### D19. Identity and Self-Image

**D19.** demonstrate a sense of identity and a positive self-image

#### D19.1

**D19.1** recognize personal interests, strengths, and accomplishments

#### D19.2

**D19.2** identify and talk about their own interests and preferences

#### D19.3

**D19.3** express their thoughts and share experiences

**Examples**

- convey their thoughts about a science discovery or about something they have made
- describe experiences at home or cultural experiences

### D20. Understanding Perspectives and Canadian Identity

**D20.** show understanding of the multiple perspectives and diversity among individuals, groups, and communities in Canada, and identify areas of connection

#### D20.1

**D20.1** understand that everyone belongs to multiple groups and communities, and that these groups and communities together shape Canadian identity

**Examples**

**Groups/communities:**

- a family, a class, a school, a neighbourhood
- a cultural or faith community

#### D20.2

**D20.2** describe, both verbally and non-verbally, ways in which they contribute to the various groups to which they belong

#### D20.3

**D20.3** demonstrate respect for different perspectives and identify areas of connection

**Examples**

- build on ideas expressed by a classmate
- adapt behaviour to accommodate a classmate’s ideas

### D21. Self-Confidence

**D21.** recognize bias in ideas and develop the self-confidence to stand up for themselves and others against prejudice and discrimination

#### D21.1

**D21.1** stand up for themselves, and apply behaviours that enhance their own and others’ well-being

**Examples**

- speak confidently
- state boundaries
- make choices to enhance their well-being and comfort

#### D21.2

**D21.2** think critically about fair/unfair and biased behaviour towards both themselves and others, and act with compassion and kindness

#### D21.3

**D21.3** recognize discriminatory and inequitable practices and behaviours and respond appropriately

### D22. People and Communities

**D22.** demonstrate an awareness of their surroundings

#### D22.1

**D22.1** recognize people in their community and talk about what they do

**Examples**

**People in their community:**

- Elder, Knowledge Keeper, store clerk, farmer, park ranger, police officer, nurse, engineer, baker

#### D22.2

**D22.2** recognize places and buildings within their community, both natural and human-made, and talk about their functions

**Examples**

**Places and buildings within their community:**

- farm, hospital, community centre

### D23. Respect and Care for Nature

**D23.** demonstrate an understanding of the natural world and the need to care for and respect the environment

#### D23.1

**D23.1** identify similarities and differences between local environments

**Examples**

**Local environments to compare:**

- a park and a pond
- a schoolyard and a field

#### D23.2

**D23.2** describe what would happen if something in the local environment changed

**Examples**

**Changes in the local environment:**

- trees in the park being cut down
- a pond drying up
- native flowers being planted in the school garden

#### D23.3

**D23.3** describe ways in which they care for and respect the environment

**Examples**

- feed the birds in winter
- reuse and recycle
- turn off unnecessary lights at home
