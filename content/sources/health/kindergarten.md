## Strand C: Self-Regulation and Well-Being

### Why is this learning important?

Your child will learn about emotions and feelings, and how to adapt to new situations. This gives them a strong foundation for life. The goal is for your child to:

- develop an understanding of how they focus and learn best
- respect themselves and others
- start to learn about physical and mental health and well-being in themselves and others

Your child will develop the ability to:

- manage their own feelings and emotions
- realize that others may have different thoughts and feelings, and to respect those differences
- deal with distractions
- advocate for themselves
- understand consequences of actions
- be aware of their physical and mental health and wellness
- participate actively and regularly in physical activities and develop movement skills

### C14. Social Skills

**C14.** identify and use social skills in play and other contexts

#### C14.1

**C14.1** act and talk with peers and adults by expressing and accepting positive messages

**Examples**

- use tone of voice and gestures appropriate to the context
- give compliments
- give and accept constructive criticism

#### C14.2

**C14.2** show the ability to take turns

**Examples**

**Occasions for taking turns:**

- while engaged in play with others
- in discussions with peers and adults

#### C14.3

**C14.3** demonstrate an awareness of ways of making and keeping friends

**Examples**

**Ways of making and keeping friends:**

- sharing, listening, talking, helping
- entering into play or joining a group

#### C14.4

**C14.4** use problem-solving skills in social situations

**Examples**

**Problem-solving skills:**

- **trial and error:** a student tries joining a game without asking, but then, seeing the response of their peers, asks politely to join
- **checking:** a student asks, “Do we need more blocks?” before adding one to the tower
- **asking for help:** a student seeks the teacher for assistance when there is a conflict amongst peers and other conflict resolution strategies are not working

### C15. Self-Regulation

**C15.** demonstrate independence, self-regulation, and a willingness to take on responsibility

#### C15.1

**C15.1** demonstrate self-reliance and a sense of responsibility

**Examples**

- take care of personal belongings
- know when to seek assistance

#### C15.2

**C15.2** demonstrate a willingness to try new experiences and to adapt to new situations

**Examples**

**New experiences to try:**

- experimenting with new materials/tools
- experimenting with writing

**New situations to adapt to:**

- having visitors in the classroom
- going on a field trip
- riding the school bus

#### C15.3

**C15.3** demonstrate self-motivation, initiative, and confidence in their approach to learning

**Examples**

- choose learning tasks independently
- persevere with challenging tasks

#### C15.4

**C15.4** demonstrate self-control and adapt behaviour to different contexts

**Examples**

**Demonstrating self-control:**

- be aware of and label their own emotions
- accept help to regulate emotions

**Adapting behaviour to different school contexts:**

- follow routines and rules in the classroom, gym, library, playground

#### C15.5

**C15.5** develop empathy for others, and acknowledge and respond to each other’s feelings

**Examples**

- tell an adult when another student is hurt/sick/upset
- have an imaginary conversation with a tree or an insect
- role-play emotions with dolls and puppets

### C16. Understanding Health Concepts

**C16.** demonstrate an awareness of their own health and well-being

#### C16.1

**C16.1** demonstrate an understanding of the effects of healthy, active living on the mind and body

**Examples**

**Healthy, active living:**

- choose a balance of active and quiet activities throughout the day
- remember to have a snack
- drink water when thirsty

#### C16.2

**C16.2** investigate the benefits of nutritious foods and explore ways of ensuring healthy eating

**Examples**

**Ways of ensuring healthy eating:**

- identifying nutritious food, including foods from various cultures
- avoiding foods to which they are allergic

#### C16.3

**C16.3** practise and discuss appropriate personal hygiene that promotes personal, family, and community health

#### C16.4

**C16.4** discuss what action to take when they feel unsafe or uncomfortable, in person or online, and when and how to seek assistance

**Examples**

**Actions to take when feeling unsafe or uncomfortable, in person or online:**

- taking action in response to inappropriate touching
- seeking assistance from an adult they know and trust, from 911 operators, or from playground monitors
- identifying substances that are harmful to the body
- when online, knowing to stop and get help from a parent or trusted adult if they feel unsafe or uncomfortable

#### C16.5

**C16.5** discuss and demonstrate in play what makes them happy and unhappy, and why

### C17. Active Participation

**C17.** participate actively and regularly in a variety of activities that require the application of movement concepts

#### C17.1

**C17.1** participate actively in creative movement and other daily physical activities

**Examples**

- dance
- engage in outdoor play and activities
- take fitness breaks

#### C17.2

**C17.2** demonstrate persistence while engaged in activities that require the use of both large and small muscles

**Examples**

**Activities that require the use of large and small muscles:**

- tossing and catching beanbags
- skipping
- lacing
- drawing and painting

#### C17.3

**C17.3** demonstrate strategies for engaging in cooperative play in a variety of games and activities

### C18. Movement Skills and Concepts

**C18.** develop movement skills and concepts as they use their growing bodies to move in a variety of ways and in a variety of contexts

#### C18.1

**C18.1** demonstrate spatial awareness in activities that require the use of large muscles

#### C18.2

**C18.2** demonstrate control of large muscles with and without equipment

**Examples**

- climb and balance on playground equipment
- roll, throw, and catch a variety of balls
- hop, slide, wheel, or gallop in the gym or outdoors

#### C18.3

**C18.3** demonstrate balance, whole-body and hand-eye coordination, and flexibility in movement

**Examples**

- walk on the balance beam
- run, jump, and climb
- catch a ball

#### C18.4

**C18.4** demonstrate control of small muscles while working in a variety of learning areas and when using a variety of materials or equipment

**Examples**

- use a functional grip when writing or drawing with a crayon or pencil
- paint with paintbrushes
- string beads or roll playdough
- cut with scissors

#### C18.5

**C18.5** demonstrate spatial awareness by doing activities that require the use of small muscles
