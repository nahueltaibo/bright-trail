## Strand D: Belonging and Contributing

### Why is this learning important?

Your child will develop a sense of self. Educators will encourage and support them to reach their full potential. They will develop social skills, self-confidence and a love for learning that they will take with them through school and life.

Your child will develop:

- a positive understanding of themselves as a person and their unique strengths
- a sense of connectedness to others, to their community and to the natural world
- ways to consider different perspectives and life experiences as well as areas of connection
- understanding of relationships and community and how people contribute to the world around them
- problem-solving skills in social situations

### D24. Responding to Art

**D24.** respond to a variety of forms of dance, drama, music, and visual arts from various cultures and communities, including using their language and mathematics knowledge and skills

#### D24.1

**D24.1** express their responses to dance and drama

**Examples**

**Respond by:**

- using movements
- making connections to their experiences with dance and drama
- describing how dance and drama make them feel

#### D24.2

**D24.2** respond to texts and art forms from various cultures and communities through classroom drama activities and make connections to their own experiences

**Examples**

**Respond by:**

- using actions, words, pictures, or props to retell a story or represent a poem
- describing their feeling about a character in a story
- sharing how they felt, using actions, pictures, or words, after listening to First Nations hand drumming, Métis fiddle music, Inuit throat singing, a song in a new language
- talking about lines, shapes and forms, colours, and textures after viewing paintings, textiles, sculptures, or illustrations

#### D24.3

**D24.3** express their responses to music by moving, by making connections to their own experiences, or by talking about the musical form

**Examples**

**Respond by:**

- clapping the patterns in the beat and rhythm of familiar music
- identifying similar beat and rhythm in different pieces of music

### D25. Exploring and Creating Art

**D25.** explore and create a variety of art forms, including dance, drama, music, and visual arts

#### D25.1

**D25.1** explore the elements of dance, drama, music, and design

**Examples**

- try out different voices for parts of a story or chant
- find different ways to move to music, trying to connect the movement with the mood and speed of the music
- create a sequence of visual patterns

#### D25.2

**D25.2** experiment with different materials, techniques, and processes used in a variety of art forms

**Examples**

- experiment with different instruments to create a rhythm pattern to accompany a familiar song
- create a sculpture, experimenting with how different materials affect its stability
- choose colours to reflect their feelings in their paintings, exploring the effects of different colours

#### D25.3

**D25.3** use problem-solving skills and imagination to create forms of art

**Examples**

- choose an alternative way to fasten their materials if the first way is unsuccessful
- contribute to making a variation on a familiar song with the class
- contribute their own ideas to role-playing

#### D25.4

**D25.4** communicate ideas through dance, drama, music, and visual arts

**Examples**

- represent their ideas about a familiar story or the meaning of a word through drawing or painting
- convey their thoughts about an experience using movement
- represent their understanding of a mathematical pattern through visual art
