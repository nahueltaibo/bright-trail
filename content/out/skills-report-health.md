## Reviewer Changes

### Grade 1

Checked all 14 Grade 1 skills for needed, direct, non-circular prerequisites, and for missing same-grade prerequisites. Every other skill's prerequisites are needed and direct, and no skill lists a redundant transitive prerequisite. Titles and descriptions are appropriate for read-aloud. One judgment call was left unchanged: g1-hlth-caring-behaviours mentions unsafe touching and consent but does not require g1-hlth-body-parts, since a kid can recognize harmful behaviour without using correct anatomical terms. The reviewer may want to reconsider this if the curriculum intends naming body parts as groundwork for discussing inappropriate touching.

The link to mental health is indirect, so the prerequisite is not truly needed and should be removed.
