## Reviewer Changes

### Grade 1

Reviewed all 42 Grade 1 skills. Apart from the one correction, the prerequisites are direct, non-redundant, not circular, and point only within Grade 1. The skills with no prerequisites are reasonable starting points. Two other possible gaps were considered and not changed: g1-sci-food-energy might benefit from g1-sci-basic-needs, since knowing that living things need food is the premise of the skill, but the skill is mainly about energy, so I left it. g1-sci-loss-effects might benefit from g1-sci-healthy-environment, but linked-nature covers the interconnection the skill depends on. Titles and descriptions are short and read-aloud friendly, so no wording changes are needed.

- `g1-sci-sun-light-heat`: This skill asks kids to describe how the amount of light and heat from the Sun changes through the day and across the seasons. The idea that the Sun gives off light and heat is the core of C2.2, which is taught by g1-sci-sun-energy. A kid who does not know the Sun warms the air, land, and water and gives light would struggle with this skill. g1-sci-sun-energy depends only on g1-sci-energy-moves, so it does not duplicate the other listed prerequisites.

### Grade 2

Reviewed all 40 proposed Grade 2 skills for real and direct prerequisites, missing prerequisites, grade ordering, self-references, redundant prerequisites, and kid-friendly wording. All prerequisites point to Grade 1 or earlier Grade 2 skills, with no later-grade or self references, and no redundant prerequisite pairs were found. Only two corrections were made, both to the water-states chain in strand C/E. Some links are judgement calls and were left alone: g2-sci-six-machines listing g2-sci-change-position, g2-sci-air-water-properties listing g1-sci-healthy-environment, and g2-sci-home-practices not listing a waste-sorting skill. Titles and descriptions read clearly for a child, though "repel" in the C2.6 title is a harder word that could be reconsidered.

- `g2-sci-three-states-water`: Sorting water as solid, liquid, or gas and describing how warming and cooling change it depends on knowing the states of water (liquid water and ice), which is already listed. Knowing where water comes from (lakes, rivers, wells, taps) is not needed for this skill, so g2-sci-water-sources is not a direct prerequisite and should be removed.
- `g2-sci-water-states`: The skill asks what makes water freeze or ice melt. A kid needs the Grade 1 idea that heat is energy that can melt ice (g1-sci-energy-moves, which explicitly covers heat melting ice) in order to understand these changes. This Grade 1 prerequisite is missing. It does not duplicate any other listed prerequisite, since g2-sci-liquid-solid-properties does not depend on it.

### Grade 3

Reviewed all 43 proposed Grade 3 skills. Every prerequisite id exists, none point to a later grade or to the skill itself, and no listed prerequisite is a duplicate of another listed prerequisite's dependency chain, except g3-sci-forces-daily (corrected above). Titles and descriptions match their expectations and read well aloud. The remaining prerequisite lists look reasonable as written, though a few are judgment calls (for example, g3-sci-plant-needs for g3-sci-plant-animal-depend), and I left them unchanged.

That is the push, pull, twist, or squeeze vocabulary taught in g3-sci-force-ways, not just the contact/non-contact sort. g3-sci-force-ways already depends on g3-sci-force-types, so listing force-types directly would be redundant. Replacing it with force-ways keeps the chain direct.

### Grade 4

Checked all 40 proposed Grade 4 skills against the Grade 1-3 skills and the Grade 4 expectations. Every prerequisite id exists in the prompt and no prerequisite points to a later grade or to the skill itself. Most skills are fine. The corrections move the Grade 2 animal adaptations prerequisite to where it is needed, add two missing same-idea prerequisites from earlier grades (plant human-impact and state conditions), and fix one awkward description. Some judgement calls were left as is, e.g. whether g1-sci-cycles or g1-sci-energy-sources would be a stronger direct prerequisite for rock cycle or light sources. Those are not clearly required.

- `g4-sci-habitat-needs`: g2-sci-animal-adaptations is not needed to learn what a habitat provides (food, water, shelter, space). Adaptations are about how body parts help survival, which is a later idea. That prerequisite belongs on g4-sci-structural-adaptations instead (see that correction). g3-sci-plant-needs is kept because it covers the needs of living things and is a direct prerequisite for the habitat idea.
- `g4-sci-structural-adaptations`: The Grade 2 skill g2-sci-animal-adaptations teaches matching animal body parts to how they help the animal survive where it lives. That is the same idea this skill extends to plants and animals in habitats, and it is missing. None of the other listed prerequisites depends on it.
- `g4-sci-human-habitat-impact`: The Grade 3 skill g3-sci-human-impact-plants covers how people's actions help or hurt plants and their homes. This is the same idea for plant habitats, which this Grade 4 skill covers, and it is missing. No other listed prerequisite depends on it.
- `g4-sci-rock-cycle`: Igneous rock forms when melted rock cools, and the rock cycle depends on heat changing materials. The Grade 2 skill g2-sci-state-conditions (heat or cold changing a liquid or solid, like chocolate melting) is the same idea and is missing. No other listed prerequisite depends on it.
- `g4-sci-community`: The current description, 'a community is different species living...', is ungrammatical and awkward to hear read aloud. The wording is fixed without changing the meaning.

### Grade 5

Checked all 40 proposed Grade 5 skills. All prerequisite ids exist in the prompt, and none point to a later grade or to the skill itself. Titles and descriptions read clearly aloud and match their expectations. Two corrections were needed. Two other possible gaps were considered and left alone because the link is only partial: g2-sci-mixtures for 'dissolving sugar' in g5-sci-physical-change, and g5-sci-chemical-change for the combustion mechanism in g5-sci-fossil-fuels-climate. The author may want to revisit these, but they are not clear errors.

- `g5-sci-matter-defined`: The skill asks kids to sort matter from non-matter. The grade 2 skill g2-sci-air-water-properties teaches that air takes up space, which is the key example kids need to see that gases are matter. Without it, the idea of 'takes up space' has no concrete anchor for air. It does not depend on any other listed prerequisite.
- `g5-sci-thermal-energy`: g1-sci-energy-moves is already reached through g5-sci-state-changes -> g2-sci-three-states-water -> g2-sci-water-states -> g1-sci-energy-moves, so listing it directly is redundant. The direct prerequisite this skill needs is g2-sci-state-conditions, which teaches that heat or cold makes a liquid or solid change. That is the same idea the grade 5 skill extends to heat absorbed or released in melting, freezing, boiling and condensing.

### Grade 6

Reviewed all 60-ish proposed Grade 6 skills against the prerequisite rules. All prerequisites point to earlier grades or to other Grade 6 skills that are not circular, and the Grade 6 expectations are covered. The three corrections address one unneeded prerequisite (interrelationships) and two missing direct Grade 3 ideas (plant parts for classification, soil components for farming). Lower-confidence items I considered but did not change: g6-sci-static-electricity linking to g5-sci-energy-forms, g6-sci-earth-moon-sun not linking to gravity for tides, and g6-sci-space-technology not linking to light-based devices for telescopes. These are judgement calls that a kid could learn without the listed links, so I left them for the curriculum author to decide.

- `g6-sci-interrelationships`: g6-sci-community-resilience is about how variety helps a community bounce back from change, which a kid does not need in order to match relationships like pollination, predator and prey, and sharing food to how they keep biodiversity going. Removing community-resilience also removes a non-direct link, since the food web is already available as a direct prerequisite.
- `g6-sci-classify-organisms`: Sorting plants into smaller groups with a classification key depends on recognizing plant parts such as seeds, cones, flowers, and leaves, which is a Grade 3 idea not reached through the current prerequisites. g3-sci-plant-parts does not depend on g4-sci-structural-adaptations, so it is a direct, non-redundant addition.
- `g6-sci-agriculture-biodiversity`: The skill explicitly includes soil life as a way biodiversity helps farms. The Grade 3 idea of living and non-living parts of soil (g3-sci-soil-components) is needed for this and is not reached through the existing prerequisites. It does not depend on the other listed prerequisites, so it is a direct addition.
