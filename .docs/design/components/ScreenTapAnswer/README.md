# ScreenTapAnswer

One question, an optional visual and 2 to 4 choice tiles. Answers on tap.

- After a tap, the right tile turns `hit` with a check, a missed tile turns calm `miss` blue with a sprout, the rest go dashed.
- The feedback panel appears at the bottom (phone) or under the tiles (landscape).
- Landscape splits question 45% and answers 55%.
