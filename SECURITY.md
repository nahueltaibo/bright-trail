# Security Policy

Found a security problem in BrightTrail? Please report it privately, not in a public issue or pull request.

## How to Report

Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting: open the repo's **Security** tab and click **Report a vulnerability**. Only the maintainer sees the report.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it. You'll get a reply as soon as the maintainer can look at it.

## What's in Scope

- The Android app and its over-the-air updates.
- The content packs the app downloads.
- The CI workflows that build, sign, and publish them.
